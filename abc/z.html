Welcome to Jade Moon’s Novels

Where updates come sporadically if at all


Bloodline

The magical skill of your clan decides your place in the caste. Follow the story of a young man who navigates the intricacies of love and power as he seeks to claim his rightful position as heir. But as secrets unravel, will he choose to continue on his path to protect his family or will he throw it all away to keep his lover?


Bonds of Time

Alex has lived his entire life feeling like there was a part of him missing. When he meets a mysterious girl, the wheels of fate start spinning to right the wrongs of millennia past.


Dragon & Phoenix

A broken country-child is taken back to his rich family in the city. Will he be able to move on? Or will his ghosts haunt him forever?


Era of Gods

Betrayed by his lover and his kingdom conquered, a prince is thrown into the intricacies of the eras and the biggest betrayal yet that has thrown the entire world out of order.


Hybrids

Children of two different species, known as Hybrids, are shunned by society and killed as soon as they are found. Follow the story of an organization of Hybrids who fight back and turn the hunters into the hunted.


Judgment

Daemons and Humans have generations of hatred, stemming from the Great War many millenia ago. But with the upcoming Aristocrats’ Assessment to choose the next ruler of the Daemons, will the scars of the past finally heal or will rifts permanently tear these two races apart?


Shadow Monarch

One is a noble child who lives shunned by his family and is eventually sold off by his father to be another noble’s spouse. The other is an agent of the monarchs, dedicated to catching the ones causing chaos throughout the kingdom. But when their hidden identities come to light, what will become of their relationship?


Spirit War

Spirits can only be contracted by noble children. But when a commoner is able to create a contract with a spirit, he is thrown into the world of high society and intrigue. Can he survive in this new environment or will his surroundings overwhelm him?

5AE

What will happen when the darkness of reality crashes with the light of dreams? Follow the tale of five friends who learn to navigate life and finally free themselves from the shackles of their past.


Blood Ties

They say that blood runs thicker than water, but when the elven prince brings his family back to the castle that he had left centuries prior, their new identities, values, and way of life clash. Will their family bonds hold them together or will their blood ties hold them back?


Finding

The progenitors of the elements are the strongest beings that control each of the ten elements. Follow this ten-in-one book as they find themselves and blossom into their new roles.

Alyssia’s Revenge

Follow the tale of a young girl who bring vengeance upon her mother’s killer: her father.
