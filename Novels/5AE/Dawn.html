⚜ Prologue ⚜

It’s said that one’s abilities depend on the quality of their soul. The higher the quality, the better the person’s basic specs. Someone with a high level soul could learn skills quicker, comprehend matters deeper, and were granted special abilities, such as magnified senses or perfect memory. But this was just the tip of the iceberg. A superior soul could influence the souls around them and increase their levels as well. 

High level souls were by no means commonplace. It would take several eons for even one soul to evolve into a high level soul. However, this was only for a natural turn of events …
