♩ Chapter 1: The Best Yet Worst FriendGuy Who Never Shuts Up ♩

The clock ticked loudly as Luca turned the pages of his book. The blue, purple, and green lights depicted on the cover were framed by the dark night sky and dots of white, shining stars scattered about.

The sound of the door unlocking and doorknob turning pulled Luca out of his book, and reflexively, he placed the book on the table beside him and corrected his posture to welcome his roommate.

Kai opened the door to see Luca sitting up straight, hands folded and resting on his lap. The two stared at each other for a few seconds before Luca stirred, turning around in his chair to pick up his book again.

“What’s wrong?” Kai asked worriedly.

Luca shook his head. “It is nothing of importance.”

Kai immediately understood what his childhood friend meant. “Old habits die hard, huh?”

Luca paused and didn’t respond, but Kai could tell Luca agreed. “Whatcha reading?”

“I am reading a book about the northern lights,” Luca replied, perfectly articulating his words in a complete sentence.

“Is it interesting?” Kai asked.

Luca paused. “It is busywork.”

“If it’s busy work, then that means you’re free right?” Kai interpreted. 

“ … That is correct?” Luca responded in confusion.

“Then play this game with me!” Kai exclaimed.

“Kai, you know I cannot — ” [Luca]

Luca‘s words were cut off by Kai. “Your mother won’t find out about this.”

“But … ” [Luca]

“Please~” Kai pleaded. “I want to play with a friend.”

“Do you not play with your boyfriend?” [Luca]

“He’s busy.” [Kai]

Kai’s aforementioned boyfriend was Leo Kennen, the youngest son of the CEO of The Canyon Corporation. He was assisting his older siblings, working at the company, leaving minimal amount of time to play with Kai.

Under Kai‘s half-persuasion, half-pleading, Luca relented and chose to play with Kai.

“So what game is it?” [Luca]

Kai shifted slightly. “It’s the VRMMORPG Legend of Elle, called Elle for short.”

Luca furrowed his brows slightly. “The name sounds familiar,” he muttered quietly. His eyes widened as he recalled the game he had quit two years prior. “ELLE?” he asked incredulously. He quickly corrected himself. “This game is ELLE?”

Kai smiled sheepishly. “Yes?”

Luca’s face fell, but he quickly schooled his expression back into neutrality. His narrowing eyes were the only feature that betrayed his annoyance.

“You already agreed to play with me; no take backs,” Kai quickly reminded him.

Luca’s eyes narrowed even more. He debated ignoring his own acceptance of Kai’s offer versus following through in what he said. It was a very close battle, but in the end, his conscience prevailed. He sighed in exasperation, inwardly muttering about how he lost, though his forlorn expression only served to make Kai’s smug grin even smugger (if that was even possible). 

Luca gave Kai a glare, to which the latter laughed at, before he dug through the endless boxes in his side of the dorm. With a bright, and infinitely annoying smile on his face, Kai handed Luca a clear glass band. 

Luca inspected the device. It was a completely smooth object, with no sharp edges, even as the band curled over itself to form the inner band. Its shape was akin to a jadeite bracelet, simply supersized to fit a person’s head. 

“What is this?” Luca asked. 

Kai grinned. “It’s the VR gear needed to play Elle.”

Luca touched his chin in thought. “I thought ELLE was a computer game.”

Kai laughed. “Ah, I guess I didn’t explain this. ELLE, short for EverLasting League of Eternity, was the computer game. Elle, which is short for Legend of Elle, is just ELLE converted into a VR game and the game that I’m inviting you to play.”

Luca stared at Kai blankly. “I cannot comprehend your words.”

Kai snickered. It was very rare seeing Luca bluntly voice out any confusion, no doubt due to his mother’s influence. “You don’t have to get it. You just need to know that this is the improved version of ELLE.” 

Luca gave his childhood friend a cold stare, begrudgingly accepting his suspicious explanation. 

“So how does this work?” Luca asked, effectively choosing to focus on the device in front of him and not on Kai’s completely unhelpful explanation. 

Kai plucked the device out of Luca’s hands and twisted it around so Luca could see the different angles of the band. “This is the front of the headgear, and this is the back,” Kai said, pointing at opposite sides of the band. “The front is the part that goes in front of the eyes.” 

Since all sides of the device looked exactly the same, Luca honestly had no clue how Kai knew which was the front or the back, but he decided to trust the master. But he would never tell Kai that to his face; he didn’t need his ego stroked even more. Besides, Luca would investigate the device later on his own time. 

Kai tapped the front of the band twice. “The front part is a touchscreen. You can calibrate your account and bind it from the settings.” 

Neither Luca’s face nor his body showed any signs of his confusion, but the two had known each other for most of their lives, so Kai could tell when Luca was confused. He explained, “You can bind your pupils and fingerprints to your account so that even if someone has your account’s username and password, they still can’t access your account. Also, if you bind your account to a fingerprint and iris, you can only access your account through this specific headgear.”

“That is to say, if one does not bind their pupil and fingerprint to a specific account, they will be able to access their account from any Virtual Reality device?” [Luca]

“Any device that is not already bound to an account,” Kai clarified. “And if you want to trade or sell a bound account, you can go to a store and register the buyer’s iris and fingerprint as the owner. And if you lose the headgear, you can go to the store to get another helmet bound to your account. You still have to buy the head gear, though.” 

“Is that so.” [Luca]

“That is so,” Kai replied. “So do you want to bind your account or not?” 

“For now, I am unable to see any downsides to binding my account, but is there any way to remove a fingerprint and pupil binding?” [Luca]

“You mean without transferring the account to another person? Yeah. But you still have to go to a store for that.” [Kai]

“And this store you keep mentioning refers to … ” [Luca]

“Hm? Oh, it’s a store called Elle’s Shop that deals with anything Elle related: merch, customer service, anything and all of the above.” [Kai]

“I understand. Then I shall choose to bind my account,” Luca replied. 

“I thought you might say that,” Kai replied with a smile. “Just put your hand here, look here, and don’t blink.” 

Luca followed Kai’s directions before wondering, “What about fingerprints left on the device?” 

“Hmm?” Kai asked. “What about them?” 

“Since this is an all-glass device, then wouldn’t there be fingerprints left all over the device? Would that not look visually unappealing?” [Luca]

“It’s actually a type of crystal,” Kai corrected. “Specifically customized to make Elle headgears. And Elle’s Shop sells wipes that work specifically to clean off stains from the gear.” 

“And I am assuming that these wipes are the most popular product at Elle’s Shop,” [Luca]

“Actually, it’s the second most popular. The most popular is the signed character posters of the top players.” [Kai]

“Signed by whom?” [Luca]

“By the characters’ IRL players, of course. Epistula, the company that owns Elle, hired the players to continue playing, attend events, sign merch, etc.” 

Luca frowned. “They get paid to play games,” he mused quietly. 

Kai snickered. “Rather, they’re actually getting paid to retain their fame.” 

“Retain their fame within the game. It does not matter what type of sophistry you choose to use; they are still being paid to play a game.” 

“Is it not a respectable occupation?” Kai asked coldly, mimicking the way Luca’s mother spoke. 

Luca froze slightly before turning away. 

Normally, Kai would’ve let such a statement slide, but he was in a much less merciful mood. Not towards Luca, of course, but towards his mother, Adelaide. So he continued, “According to your mother, anyone in the entertainment industry, or those who do hard labor don’t have a respectable job; only those who work in a company like her have respectable jobs.” The last two words of his sentence were emphasized mockingly. “Are you going to be like her as well? Work a respectable job? Discriminate against those who don’t? Try to control everyone’s life? Piss off your partner so much that they can’t stand you and end up leaving? Why don’t you just be the continuation of your mother’s life, then?” 

“Kane Kaizer Irie!” Luca intoned coldly. “Don’t go too far!” 

“Oh, look, you sound like your mother already.” [Kai]

A tense atmosphere filled the room as the two glared at each other coldly. Finally, Kai backed away, saying, “Sorry, I said too much.” 

Luca sighed, turning his head away and speaking quietly, “I know what you’re doing, Kai, but please don’t try to pull me away from my mother. She doesn’t mean badly.” 

“I don’t know what you see in her,” Kai snorted. He finished fiddling with the headgear and handed it back to Luca. “The rest of your account setup is done in the game itself.” 

“Thank you,” Luca smiled weakly. “Does this game have True Names as well?” 

Kai nodded. “Yup. Also, old accounts from ELLE can be reactivated by their respective users in the game, as long as you find the bodies of your old accounts and answer the verification questions. The accounts will usually be left where you last logged off, but if your account had been good friends with an NPC, then the system had the NPC take the body back.” Kai smirked. “Just another fail-safe to be able to find the body to begin with.” 

“How is the owner of the body confirmed?” Luca asked. “Not everyone would remember their account credentials, and there may be fanatics who remember everything about the idols’ accounts and may attempt to impersonate their idols.” 

Kai smiled happily. “When you find an account, you can choose to revive it or not. I’ve found some old friends from their playstyle, but they’ve chosen not to revive their account and to leave it as a trophy. However, if you do choose to revive the account, you have to answer all of the verification questions correctly or your account will be permanently deleted.” 

“What if the account is successfully revived? Does the current account need to be discarded?” Luca asked. 

Kai shook his head. “Elle has a multi-account system where one player can be bound to multiple accounts. It’s a feature for the more famous players who don’t want to be mobbed by their fans. In the system, it’s known as Alias. The main accounts are known as Official Identity, Offi for short.” 

“The storyline says that side-accounts are Aliases of the Official Identities?” Luca mused, aware of ELLE’s focus on a logical storyline. “Can the Aliases be accessed while in the Official Identity?”

Kai nodded. “It can. Once you bind your accounts to your fingerprint and pupil, then you can sway between your accounts without having to log out. You just have to say {Switch} and the name of the account you want to swap to, and you can swap accounts.” 

“How convenient,” Luca remarked. 

Kai smiled happily, eyes shining proudly like a child showing off his work. “And when you add a new account to your player profile, you have to designate one of your accounts as the Offi and the others as the Alias. But if one of your accounts has a morality of less than 15%, then you are going to be put on the wanted list.”

Luca nodded in understanding, as this system had also been present in ELLE. The wanted list was a bounty hunter list where the stronger the player and the lower the morality level, the higher the reward one could receive when defeating them and turning them into the authorities. Of course, the player could then also attempt to break out of prison, which would lead to the bounty being even higher, but that was a different story. 

“Morality levels increase as one levels up, correct?” Luca confirmed. 

“Yup. Though, since the multi-account system didn’t exist in ELLE, there is something added. If the Offi of Aliases was to be found out, then morality levels of the Aliases would synchronize with the Offi.

“And how do they change?” [Luca]

“It’s the same as in ELLE: do good deeds to increase morality and commit crimes to decrease morality,” Kai suddenly thought of something, “Right, and there’s a marriage system, which — ”

Luca sighed. He was really regretting his current decision. Kai was just going to regurgitate all of this information that was probably never going to be needed, not caring if anyone was actually listening to him.

♩ Chapter 2: Manifestation I ♩

To sum up Kai’s long-winded explanation, the marriage system effectively bound multiple in-game friends together. By marrying in-game, all parties could get benefits, including automatic teleportation to your partner’s location, shared inventory, and a stat boost when your partner was near you. However, when you marry someone, your character’s true name would be known to your partner(s).

In the game, having your true name known — by anyone — was highly dangerous. Other players could permanently curse a player to debuff them —increase cooldown times, lowering crit rates, constantly lowering HP/MP, making spells cost much more MP, etc. — as long as they used their true name and a special curse paper. One could temporarily clear a true name curse by purifying it, but the only way to permanently cleanse a true name curse would be to change your true name, which could only be done with a true name prop, which was about as rare as the curse paper itself. 

Therefore, even though players could marry an unlimited number of people with any gender or race, most wouldn’t marry others indiscriminately, especially high-ranking players. Also, when an Alias was decided, the Alias’ and Official Identity’s True Names would merge into the True Name of the Official Identity. 

Luca stared at him. “Are you done yet?”

“Not even a little,” Kai replied, a mischievous grin on his face.

Luca promptly turned his seat around and re-opened his book. 

Kai snickered happily. “Okay, okay; I’ll shut up now. So, you want to create your account now?” 

Luca gave Kai the glare that he had perfected over the years — one that barely skirted the line of what Adelaide considered rude, yet one that was cold enough to strike fear into those on the receiving end — and relented. Kai was thick-skinned and shameless, able to out-argue practically anyone. There was no point in trying to debate with him and waste energy. 

Luca stood up and took the VR headgear from Kai. 

“You should lie down,” Kai advised. “Since you’ll be motionless while in the game, you should put yourself in a comfortable position while you play.” 

Luca listened and lay down on his back on his bed. According to Kai’s instructions, Luca put the device on his head and closed his eyes. 

When he opened his eyes again, he was in a pure white space. A translucent blue box appeared in front of him, floating in the air. 

【Welcome to Legend of Elle.】

【Please enter your username.】

Luca entered [Spinnea] into the box. 

【Username is [Spinnea]. Please confirm.】

Luca clicked the light blue ‘Confirm’ button. 

【Username has been confirmed.】

【Please enter your password.】

Luca typed a random string of characters into the box before clicking ‘show password’. He took a glance at it before clicking ‘enter’. Able to perfectly recall anything he had seen or heard even just once before, he really didn’t have to create a memorable password. 

【Please re-enter your password.】

Luca carelessly re-typed the intelligible string of characters into the box and clicked ‘Confirm’. 

【Password has been confirmed.】

【Character customization is commencing.】

【Please enter your character’s true name. Click here to learn about true names.】

Luca already knew what true names were and didn’t bother clicking on the link. He simply typed in [Aurora Borealis] into the box.

【Your true name will be [Aurora Borealis]. Please confirm. Warning! True names cannot be altered after confirming!】

【True name confirmed.】

【Please enter your character’s nickname.】

Nicknames were the names that other players would see and that NPCs would use to refrain from using others’ True Names. 

Luca typed in the more commonly known version of his true name, [Northern Lights].

【Nickname confirmed.】

【Please choose your core.



  

    Citrine
    Aquamarine
    Garnet
    Diamond
    Emerald
    Amethyst
  

  

    Increases HP by 10%
    Increases MP by 10%
    Increases Strength by 10%
    Increases Vitality by 10%
    Increases Agility by 10%
    Increases all stats by 5%
  




Click here to learn about cores.】

Cores had also existed in ELLE, but Kai hadn’t mentioned anything about them, so Luca clicked on the link curiously. 

【Each core grants a different buff to the player’s character. Having a core destroyed is one of the few ways to truly die in Elle. Read the Elle official website to learn more.】

Luca browsed through the core selection before selecting {Aquamarine}.

【Please confirm {Aquamarine} as your core. Warning! Core cannot be altered after confirming!】

【Core confirmed.】

【Please style your character.】

Countless options for appearances appeared in front of Luca. Starting from the top, Luca picked ‘male’ for his character’s gender before setting his character’s height and weight. Starting stupidly at the color wheel in front of him, Luca wordlessly adjusted the dials and gave his character coffee colored hair and silver eyes. 

Luca then adjusted the hair length and every other feature of his character’s face until he was satisfied with his character’s appearance. Glancing at the clock in the corner of the screen, Luca saw that nearly an hour had passed. He touched his nose in embarrassment as he realized exactly how much time had passed. It was an occupational illness from being Adelaide’s son that everything he did had to be perfect, even if he was just setting up his game character’s appearance. 

Finally, Luca clicked ‘Confirm’.

【Character appearance confirmed.】

【Character data saved.】

【Game loading. Please wait momentarily … 】

【Welcome to Legend of Elle, Northern Lights!】

A bright flash of light covered his sight and Luca closed his eyes. When he opened them again, he honestly couldn’t tell the difference between having his eyes opened or closed. His surroundings were pitch black, and he was unable to move. The game window popped up in front of him again. 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 1. Would you like to open this quest?】

Luca clicked ‘Yes.’ 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 1
You are a newborn nymph, unable to move or sense your surroundings. Absorb mana to infuse energy into your core. 

Mana absorbed: 0/70,000

Time Limit: None
Reward: Being able to see
Penalty: Being blind forever】

Luca was speechless. At least it’s straightforward? A gentle wind blew past his ears, the sun shining dimly — enough to feel its warmth, yet not a sweltering heat — and birds chirping, a wordless lullaby. Taking deep breaths, he allowed the warmth to gradually enter and spread around his body. It had been a while since Luca had felt so relaxed. Normally, as soon as he finished one thing, his mother would throw three other things at him to master, so he was always busy. Enjoying the few moments of rest that he could get, Luca closed his eyes and unknowingly fell asleep.

♩ Chapter 3: Manifestation II ♩

When Luca woke up, his mind was incredibly groggy. His head pounding and eyesight blurry, Luca attempted to sit up before remembering that he was a jewel right now; he couldn’t! Then, his muddled brain finally reacted: he could see! 

When his eyesight finally cleared, Luca took a look at his surroundings. He could see all 360° around him, but he couldn’t move his body. Different gems dotted the plain all around him. As he looked closer, he could see his own reflection in the clear facets of the diamond beside him. A beautiful teardrop-shaped aquamarine crystal was reflected there. I suppose that’s me, Luca thought. 

A ding! interrupted his thoughts. 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 1 has been completed! Would you like to open the next quest?】

Luca clicked ‘Yes.’ 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 2
You have absorbed enough mana into your core to sense your surroundings. However, you are still unable to move. Absorb elemental mana to infuse energy into your core. 

Fire mana absorbed: 0/50,000
Earth mana absorbed: 0/50,000
Wind mana absorbed: 0/50,000
Water mana absorbed: 0/50,000

Time Limit: None
Reward: Being able to move
Penalty: Forever being unable to move】

Once he accepted the quest, four different colored glowing streams of light appeared in the air. They moved around in wisps that constantly connected and detached again. Luca noticed that the red and blue and the yellow and green strands repelled each other, and the red and yellow and the blue and green strands intertwined closely, but never connected. The other colors didn’t interact in any specific way: sometimes they were close, sometimes they were far, sometimes they got tangled in each other and unraveled themselves. 

Enraptured, Luca reached out a hand to the red light. His hand felt warm. His counter went up.【Fire mana absorbed: 1/50,000】. 

Luca then touched the blue, green, and yellow mana, and was notified that water, wind, and earth mana was collected, respectively. The water mana was moist, wind was dry, and earth was grainy. 

Luca observed the mana as he collected them. Hesitantly, he attempted to mix the water and wind mana. He couldn’t physically connect them, as he would just end up collecting the mana if he touched it. He thought about making the strands connect into one string. He felt a very small pressure on his head before the strands fused, turning into a light blue one. When he did, he was notified that his collected water and wind mana went down by one. 

When he touched the light blue strand, it felt cold to the touch. It dissipated, but the water and wind mana counter didn’t increase. Luca narrowed his eyes. When the Water + Wind mana dissipated, it had the same feeling as when he had previously collected the fire, water, wind, and earth mana separately. He wondered, could it be that the mana is collecting, but it falls under a different type, so it doesn’t show up on the wind and water counter? And the new counter isn’t shown … He didn’t know how much to collect, so he simply decided to collect 100x the required amount for all types of mana. 

As Luca continued collecting mana with one hand, the other merged the mana strands together before collecting them as well. At first, his rate of absorption was much faster than his rate of consumption, causing him to have to pause his absorption to consume more, but as he became more proficient at merging the mana, the labels of mana collected fluctuated in a constant pattern. 

Luca merged every possible combination of mana he could think of: Fire + Earth mana resulted in a metallic grey mana that was hard yet flexible; Fire + Wind became a neon-yellow, practically green colored mana thread that granted an electric shock when he touched it; and Earth + Water resulted in a forest green color and was soft and slightly moist. 

When Luca tried to combine Fire and Water mana together, the strands immediately repelled before dispersing. Perhaps they are not strong enough to hold together, Luca mused and combined ten strands of Fire mana and ten strands of Water mana together. When he tried to merge them again, the two strands repelled for a while before finally merging. 

Luca’s expression brightened. The new mana strand was a blue-violet color and floated around Luca before he absorbed it. Luca continued to combine Earth and Wind mana using the same technique and created a gray-colored mana that felt heavy to the touch. After that, Fire + Earth + Wind mana resulted in a light grey mana that made a popping sound as it was collected. Fire + Earth + Water became a white mana that held no other specks of color in its threads. Fire + Wind + Water became a deep purple color that made his head hurt when he touched it. Finally, Earth + Wind + Water became a light yellow mana strand that made him feel refreshed and relaxed. 

When Luca tried to merge the four different elemental mana together, all of the strands repelled before dispersing. Luca furrowed his eyebrows and tried again. The same result occurred. Luca then tried to merge Fire + Earth together before merging Water + Wind together and then merging the two combinations together. However, nothing special happened. The strands simply passed through each other as if nothing had happened. The same result happened when Fire + Wind was combined with Water + Earth. 

But, when Fire + Water and Earth + Wind was tried, Luca received a lessened, yet still frightful amount of resistance, and the strands still dissipated. Luca tried combining ten strands of the two mana threads together (100 of each element in all). The strands held for a few seconds longer, Luca noted, before dissipating once more. 

Luca continued to add more strands in his experiment, but the more strands he tried to keep merged, the more focus it required. Luca was forced to stop absorbing mana to dedicate all of his attention to the task at hand. 

Merge, merge, dissipate.

Combine, combine, disperse.

Finally, when 1000 strands of each elemental mana (100 strands of gray and deep purple) mana had been combined, the strands finally merged, forming a long, black strand. It seemed as if it could suck in anything around it. 

Luca breathed a sigh of relief. His head hurt after all that concentrating, but he finally managed to figure out all of the mana combinations. He continued to absorb mana with one hand and merge them with the other hand, slowly growing more proficient and he could do it with ease. 

When he had collected 50,000 strands of Fire, Earth, Wind, and Water mana, the system window popped up again. 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 2 has been completed! Would you like to open the next quest?】

Luca tilted his head. Taking two seconds to decide, he ignored the window and moved it to the side of his field of vision to continue absorbing mana. Only when he reached five million for every type of mana was Luca satisfied and he pulled the system window back over to click ‘Yes.’

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 3
You have learned to absorb the mana around you — an essential skill for a nymph. Your next task is to move this mana throughout your body.

Mana pathways activated: 0/10

Time Limit: None
Reward: Being able to wield magic
Penalty: Forever being unable to wield magic】

Luca read the quest a few more times before closing his eyes. He recalled the sensation of merging the mana threads before locating that sensation within his body. A gentle warmth was gathered in the center of his core. It was cooler than the fire mana, but it was a comfortable heat, one that held the feeling of home. 

Luca smiled derisively. He had never known the feeling of home, yet he had instinctively understood it when he felt it. He quickly shook his head, ridding his mind of any unnecessary thoughts. 

Focusing on that warmth in his core, he spread it all throughout the jewel. Perhaps it was due to having previously manipulated the man strands, but circulating them around his body was an extremely simple feat. It didn’t take long for the quest to be completed. 

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 3 has been completed! Would you like to open the next quest?】

【Chain Quest: Manifestation 4
You have learned how to manipulate mana — the most essential skill in invoking magic. Now, you can finally create your body! Follow the mana veins in your core and extend them to create your body.

Body Completion: 0%

Time Limit: None
Reward: Obtain a humanoid body
Penalty: Staying as a jewel forever】

Luca snickered, choosing to ignore the overly blunt description of the quest. The words reminded him of a certain childhood friend of his. As he finished reading the quest, he saw very faint white lines that seemed to outline the figure of a human body. When Luca directed the mana stored in his core through the lines, they lit up with a multi-colored hue. After all the lines that outlined the body were filed, nothing else happened. No ‘Quest Completed!’ message popped up, and the mana lines floating in the air didn’t disappear either. 

Luca curiously tilted his head to the side. He was pretty sure that he had filled out all of the outlines to their maximum capacity — blame his mother for that — so he was sure he hadn’t missed a spot. So where had he gone wrong? 

Even as he was pondering, Luca’s grasp over the mana hadn’t loosened at all. However, he did feel a slight resistance as the mana strands tried to move into the outline. Cautiously, Luca allowed some more mana to leave and fill in the outline. The mana, Luca noticed, didn’t dissipate, unlike what happened when the mana was allowed to move outside of the outline. 

Luca nodded and carefully filled in his outline, packing in the outline so densely that no light could be seen peeking through the drawing. A quarter way through, Luca perceived that he was running out of mana, so he hurriedly absorbed more. In this way, Luca quickly accomplished his task in less than half an hour.
