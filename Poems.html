Number 2

Crafted in wood, painted in yellow
A sharp metal band fastens the rubber on your head
A harsh blade cuts at your feet
Yet you do not cry nor scream
You merely take the pain
The numbing pain that repeats again and again
Your head is rubbed bare, your feet broken beyond repair
But still you trudge on, completing your quest to assist in putting words to paper
Some find it funny to break you in half
Some find it amusing to pluck off your hair
Some find it joyous to strip you of your flesh, baring your bones to the world before grinding even those to dust
But still your soldier on, baring all the pain, gritting your teeth, but never losing to despair
But even after your every sacrifice, consuming your entire body for the sake of some ungrateful brat
You are still abandoned in the end
For the fancier barrel
For the more convenient spring
For the replaceable bones sold separately at some 99 cent store
For the fancy decorations added to the plastic surfaces of these tools
Thrown in the garbage, are you
Less useful, are you
Years of dedication traded for the newest fad, are you
Losing light, are you
Forever number 2, are you

The Final Dream

By: Jade Horse

If your intentions are pure and your heart
Is true, Mother Nature will come help you,
Everyone says, but not for me, my dreams
Within them the Nightmare World comes alive.

Every night it’s the same vicious cycle,
Climb into bed and wait … the clock ticks.
The spirits come out softly to greet me
As I lose myself in sleep and terror.

I wake up in the forest with a chill.
I look around but everything is still.
No movement, no animals, no people,
Deep in the wilderness with the trees and grass.

The moonlight reflecting on the water, 
Gentle and warm is the wind on my face.
Mother Nature almost tricked me — again,
The piercing Wolf cry — my dreams are over.

Creating and moaning as trees come to life,
Branches get closer, the wind gets colder.
Shadows keep coming, more and more of them,
To remind me of those who’ve been taken. 

As they branch up, the chill goes up my spine;
The darkest of all shadows approaches
In the mist. Next to him, that snow white Wolf!
A new wave of terror comes with that cry.

The once dead birds come back alive and speak;
They have the face of my friend, who they killed.
Their voice becomes his, as my tears fall down,
You’ll be okay, my friend and then he’s gone.

My dad was next, I thought I didn’t care;
He screams for help but I stay standing there.
The birds come back to finish eating him
While I watch with pain, full of screams and tears.

Waiting for that cry, but it doesn’t come.
I can’t take it anymore, send me home!
Sitting down by a pond, waiting for torture,
Cautiously stare — my face in the water.

I stand up, hearing the Wolf’s piercing cry;
The water turns to ice, waiting for him.
Once again I stare at my reflection;
I see a killer looking back at me.

The Wolf stands next to me in the cold ice,
His angelic reflection shines within.
How can one like you look like that? I asked,
Stay here and fight for all your peace, I heard.

Going back to ace those dead birds I feared,
My friend, my dad, especially myself.
Conquering those fears put peace in my heart;
The Wolf smirked at me, and then he was gone.

My only regret: listening to Him.
If your intentions are pure and your heart
Is true, Mother Nature will come help you.
I can never wake up to say not true.
